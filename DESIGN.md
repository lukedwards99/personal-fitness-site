# Three local directions

The user explicitly requested three complete alternatives and chose direct code implementation. The current directions are Editorial, Split and Logbook. All directions are equally complete, and selection happens through the running gallery rather than a preliminary approval board.

## Exploration
Audience: readers looking for useful training ideas and an honest personal account of bodybuilding. The mechanism is one trainer sharing both the methods and the lived practice. The predictable category rut is a generic coaching sales funnel or a fitness dashboard; neither suits this publishing website.
Grounded candidates: independent training magazine; handwritten training field journal; athletic event poster; contemporary strength club publication; photo documentary archive; sports science handbook; long-form personal essay collection. Seed a7b25910 assigned the strength club publication. This is expressed in the editorial direction; the field journal and athletic poster supply the requested contrasting alternatives.
Catalog challengers were declined on product clarity and audience fit: cracktro queue, film bench, tensegrity network, alphabet weather, construction-grid specimen and console rooms. The useful discipline carried forward is full palette commitment, readable chronology, strong type hierarchy, precise measurement typography, and purpose-led navigation. No ornamental interfaces displace reading.

## Editorial — /editorial
Independent strength club publication. Warm chalk paper, near-black ink, vermilion, DM Sans and Barlow Condensed. An oversized magazine headline is paired with a full height equipment photograph, then useful articles and a dated journal. Strong horizontal rules, broad margins, asymmetric images. Readable article pages share the world.

## Revision — October 17, 2026
Owner feedback on the first round: the drafts felt busy, the two main sections were hard to find, an About section isn't needed on the main page, and a more minimalist feel is preferred. Editorial was the favorite and was simplified lightly: navigation and hero actions now name only Training and the journal, and the contents list, slogan strip, pull quote and About section were removed. Fieldnotes and Iron were retired (archived in the ignored `.local-review/archive-2026-10-17/`) and replaced by two new minimal directions.

## Split — /split
A training split: the site divides cleanly into two halves. The home page is two doors that fill the first screen, Training on paper and the Journal on graphite, side by side on desktop and stacked on phones. Each door shows only the section name, one line, and the newest title. The header has the name plus two links. Training and the journal each have their own page (`/split/training`, `/split/journal`), and articles inherit their half's tone. Inter Tight only, no accent color, no photography on the doors.

## Logbook — /logbook
A typed training log. Text-first and quiet: a Newsreader masthead and titles, Inter Tight UI, and IBM Plex Mono dates on warm white with a single cobalt accent. A sticky Training | Journal switch sits at the top of every page. The home page shows the newest few of each; `/logbook/training` and `/logbook/journal` hold the full, searchable lists grouped by month. No images on the index pages.

## Shared craft
Advice and journey each receive real content and direct entry points. All sample content is labeled; stock images never imply they depict Luke. Original code and assets only. Keyboard focus, labeled controls, responsive behavior, reduced motion and accessible colors. Filters produce meaningful empty states. Article links, back links and deep links work.
