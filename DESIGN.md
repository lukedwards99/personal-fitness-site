# Split site

The selected Split design is the single public site. Its home page divides into two doors: Training on paper and Journal on graphite. They sit side by side on desktop and stack on phones. Each door shows the section name, one explanatory line, and the latest title. The header contains Luke Edwards's name and the two section links.

## Visual language

Keep the established design: Inter Tight throughout, oversized tightly set headings, generous space, fine horizontal rules, and a restrained monochrome palette. Training uses paper `#f2f2ee` with graphite `#161616` text; the journal reverses those tones. The home doors contain no photographs. Section features and articles use grayscale stock photography with clear credits. Articles inherit their section's light or dark tone and retain a comfortable reading width.

## Navigation and content

Canonical routes are `/`, `/training`, `/journal`, and `/post/:slug`. Training provides category filters; Journal provides chronological entries and full-text search with clear result counts and empty states. Article pages include section back links, sharing, previous/next entries, and related reading.

Legacy `/split`, `/split/training`, `/split/journal`, and `/split/post/:slug` links redirect to the corresponding canonical paths. Retired design routes use the normal not-found page. The site has no design gallery or comparison switcher.

## Craft and integrity

Preserve keyboard focus, labeled controls, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and accessible contrast. Keep sample writing visibly labeled and stock photographs clearly distinguished from Luke's own training and appearance. The current selection keeps the existing Split layout and visual treatment.
